Privacy Policy

SimpleWorkoutGen — Effective April 28, 2026

Data Collection

SimpleWorkoutGen does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All workout history, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using the browser's localStorage (web) or the app's sandboxed storage (iOS).

Apple HealthKit

When you grant permission, the app reads and writes the following HealthKit data types:

  • Read: Body weight (for calorie estimation), heart rate (from Apple Watch), workout history
  • Write: Completed workout sessions, active energy burned

This data is stored in Apple Health on your device and is governed by Apple's privacy policies. SimpleWorkoutGen does not access HealthKit data for any purpose other than displaying it to you and saving your workouts.

Apple Watch

When paired with an Apple Watch, the app uses WatchConnectivity to sync workout state (exercise names, timer countdown, phase) between your iPhone and Apple Watch. Heart rate data collected by the watch during workouts is sent to the iPhone for display and included in the HealthKit workout record. This data never leaves your devices.

Analytics & Tracking

SimpleWorkoutGen contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no third-party services. The app does not communicate with any server.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally:

  • Workout history and session details
  • Equipment preferences
  • Theme, font size, and sound/voice settings
  • Body weight (if entered manually in Settings)

You can export all your data as a JSON file from Settings > Backup > Export, and you can delete all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Since the app collects no data, changes are unlikely.
