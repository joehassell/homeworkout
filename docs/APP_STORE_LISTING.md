# App Store Listing — SimpleWorkoutGen

## App Name

SimpleWorkoutGen

## Subtitle

Home Workout Builder & Timer

## Promotional Text

Generate custom workouts with smart warm-ups, guided timer, Apple Watch heart rate, and Apple Health integration. No account needed.

## Description

Build and run custom workouts in seconds. Choose your workout type, duration, intensity, and equipment — SimpleWorkoutGen generates a complete session with warm-up, main exercises, rest periods, and cooldown, all timed to hit your exact target duration.

WORKOUT BUILDER
• 5 workout types: Strength, HIIT, Conditioning, Functional, Yoga
• 15 to 60 minute sessions with 3 intensity levels
• Focus areas: boost or exclude muscle groups (Push, Pull, Lower, Core, Full Body, Mobility)
• 9 equipment options from bodyweight to barbell
• Smart warm-up tailored to your workout type
• 5-minute cooldown biased toward muscles you just worked
• Single-sided exercises with automatic switch-sides cues

GUIDED TIMER
• Countdown timer with audio beeps and voice coaching
• Phase-by-phase guidance: Warm-up → Work → Rest → Cooldown
• Intensity-scaled rest periods (60–180 seconds)
• Weight tracking for strength sets
• Pause, skip, restart, and navigate between exercises
• Fullscreen mode for distraction-free training

APPLE WATCH COMPANION
• See your current exercise, countdown timer, and workout phase on your wrist
• Live heart rate from watch sensors
• Pause, resume, and skip exercises from Apple Watch
• Haptic cues for phase changes, countdown, and side switches
• Works even if your iPhone screen is off

APPLE HEALTH & FITNESS
• Completed workouts automatically saved to Apple Health
• Workouts appear in the Fitness app and count toward your Activity rings
• Accurate calorie calculation using heart rate data from Apple Watch
• Body weight read from Health for precise estimates

PERSONALISATION
• 5 colour themes: Dark, Midnight, Forest, High Contrast, Light
• Adjustable font size (5 steps) for readability during workouts
• Session history with calendar heatmap and stats
• Full JSON backup and restore

YOGA
• 5 styles: Vinyasa Flow, Hatha, Yin, Power, Restorative
• ~50 poses with Sanskrit names and detailed narration
• Calm voice guides each breath and movement step by step
• Gentle singing-bowl chimes between poses
• Centering breath at start, Savasana to close every practice

PRIVACY FIRST
No accounts. No cloud. No tracking. All your data stays on your device.
